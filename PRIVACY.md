# Privacy Policy

*Last updated: October 25, 2026*

ARC Chat is a free, open source text chat for ARC Raiders. This page explains what data the app and the ARC Chat server handle, and why.

## On your computer

- **Screenshots of the game window** are used only to read the match code, with Windows' built-in text recognition. They never leave your computer, and the temporary image is deleted right after it is read.
- **Your settings** (colors, hotkeys, window position, muted players) are saved on your computer.
- **Your session token** is saved on your computer, encrypted with your Windows account.

The app contains no analytics, no ads and no tracking.

## On the ARC Chat server

When you link your Steam account, you sign in on Steam's own website. ARC Chat never sees your Steam password.

| Data | Why | How long |
|---|---|---|
| Your SteamID, Steam name and avatar link | To show your name in the chat and recognize your account | Until your session ends (30 days at most, or when you unlink) |
| Your session token, stored as a one-way hash | To keep you signed in | Same as above |
| Your chat messages | To show them to the players in your match | In memory only, deleted at the latest 2 hours after the room is empty |
| Reports you send or receive (names, the match code, the reason and the reported player's last messages) | So moderators can review them | 30 days |
| Mutes and bans | To apply moderation decisions | Until the penalty ends or is lifted |
| Your IP address | To limit spam and abuse | In memory only, a few minutes. Standard server logs may also contain it |

Other players in your match see your Steam name and your messages. They don't see your SteamID: the chat uses an identifier derived from it that can't be turned back into your Steam profile.

Your Steam name and avatar are read from Steam's public API. Nothing is sold or shared with anyone else, and nothing is used for advertising.

## Your choices

- **Unlink your Steam account** in the app's settings: your session is deleted from the server right away.
- **Uninstall the app** to remove everything stored on your computer.
- To ask about your data or have it deleted, open an issue at https://github.com/JaysonPasquier/ARC-Chat/issues or email jaysonpasquier.contact@gmail.com.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page, with its date.
